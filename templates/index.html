Frequently Asked Questions

What is this downloader?

Dadaw is a modern web interface powered by the robust open-source yt-dlp library. It allows you to download videos from hundreds of platforms easily.

Why isn't my URL working?

The video might be private, age-restricted, or hosted on a platform that is not currently supported by yt-dlp.

Why are some qualities unavailable?

We only show qualities that are actually available on the source platform. We cannot upscale a 720p video to 1080p.

Why is FFmpeg required for some downloads?

Many modern platforms (like YouTube) store high-quality video and audio separately. FFmpeg is used to merge these separate tracks into a single, high-quality file.

Does it support audio-only downloads?

Yes, you can select the "Audio Only" option to extract and download just the audio track.

Do I need an account?

No, there is no signup or login required to use this service.
